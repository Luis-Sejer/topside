# Changelog

All notable changes to Topside are documented here. The format is based on
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and Topside follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Every app shares one version number.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Awake: Cover Screen blacks out every display and shuts out the keyboard and mouse while the Mac stays awake and signed in, so work in progress keeps running. Touch ID or your password uncovers it.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Week: the week number in the menu bar, and a month calendar with the weeks.
- Awake: keeps the Mac and its display awake for 15 minutes to 4 hours, or until you turn it off.
- Pipette: click, pick any pixel, and its hex code is copied. Right-click for recent colors as hex, RGB or SwiftUI.
- Mirror: your camera, mirrored, for a look before a call. The camera runs only while it's open.
- Upnext: a countdown to your next meeting, with its Teams, Zoom or Meet link one click away.
- Shelf: drop files on the icon to park them, and drag them out wherever they're going.
- Scrub: strips tracking parameters (utm_source, fbclid, si…) from links as you copy them.
- Zones: your team's local times, with one city pinned to the menu bar.
- Beam: the clipboard as a QR code, so your phone's camera can open the link.
- Hush: mutes the microphone itself, so it works in every call app. Click or press ⌃⌥M.
- Drawer: a bar with the menu bar icons macOS 27 hides behind its » button, and a search. Click its chevron or press ⌃⌥B, then click an icon to open its menu. Drawer needs macOS 27; the other apps run on macOS 26.
- Every app checks GitHub for a new version and offers it in its right-click menu, with Check for Updates… there too.
- After an update, each app shows what's new in that version once.
